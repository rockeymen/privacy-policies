## 中文（简体）

本隐私政策说明 **TimeCam**（以下简称“本应用”，由 StellarLink 开发）如何收集、使用与保护你的信息。本应用是一款延时摄影（缩时）工具，所有照片与视频均在你的设备本地处理。

### 1. 我们收集的信息

**1.1 匿名分析与崩溃数据（自动收集）**
为了改进产品稳定性与体验，本应用会使用 Google Firebase 收集以下**匿名**数据：
- **崩溃数据**（通过 Firebase Crashlytics）：应用崩溃时的技术信息，如设备型号、系统版本、崩溃堆栈。
- **使用数据**（通过 Firebase Analytics）：如功能使用情况、会话时长、界面交互等汇总统计。

上述数据**不与你的身份关联**，不包含你的姓名、邮箱、手机号，也不包含任何照片或视频内容。

**1.2 我们“不”收集的信息**
- 本应用**不会上传、存储或传输你的任何照片、视频或文件名**。所有拍摄、导入、对齐与合成均在你的设备本地完成。
- 本应用不申请定位权限，不收集你的地理位置。
- 本应用不接入任何广告 SDK，不使用 IDFA / 广告标识符，因此不会向你展示个性化广告，也不会用于跨应用跟踪。

### 2. 第三方服务
本应用使用 **Google Firebase**（Analytics 与 Crashlytics）来收集上述匿名数据。相关数据由 Google 按其隐私政策处理。详见：https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

### 3. 数据的使用
我们仅将收集到的匿名数据用于：
- 排查崩溃、提升应用稳定性；
- 了解功能使用情况，改进产品体验。

我们**不会**将这些数据用于广告定向或跨应用跟踪。

### 4. 你的选择与权利
- **关闭遥测**：你可以在本应用「设置」中关闭匿名分析与崩溃上报，关闭后我们将停止收集此类数据。
- **删除你的数据**：由于照片与视频始终保存在你的设备本地，卸载本应用即可彻底删除相关素材；匿名遥测数据不与你身份关联，无需单独删除。

### 5. 儿童隐私
本应用年龄分级为 **4+**，并非专门面向 13 岁以下儿童设计，也不会在应用中收集儿童的个人信息。

### 6. 数据安全
你的照片与视频始终保存在设备本地，本应用不会将其上传至任何服务器。匿名遥测数据通过加密连接（HTTPS）传输至 Firebase。

### 7. 政策变更
如本隐私政策发生变更，我们将在本页面更新并注明生效日期。

### 8. 联系我们
如有任何疑问，请通过邮箱 **privacy@stellarlink.app** 联系我们。

## English

This Privacy Policy explains how **TimeCam** (the “App”, developed by StellarLink) collects, uses, and protects your information. TimeCam is a time-lapse camera tool, and all photos and videos are processed entirely on your device.

### 1. Information We Collect

**1.1 Anonymous Analytics and Crash Data (collected automatically)**
To improve stability and experience, the App uses Google Firebase to collect the following **anonymous** data:
- **Crash data** (via Firebase Crashlytics): technical information such as device model, OS version, and crash stacks.
- **Usage data** (via Firebase Analytics): aggregated statistics such as feature usage, session length, and interface interactions.

This data is **not linked to your identity** and does not include your name, email, phone number, or any photo or video content.

**1.2 Information We Do NOT Collect**
- The App **does not upload, store, or transmit any of your photos, videos, or file names**. All capture, import, alignment, and composition happen locally on your device.
- The App does not request location permission and does not collect your geographic location.
- The App does not integrate any advertising SDK, does not use IDFA / advertising identifiers, and therefore shows no personalized ads and is not used for cross-app tracking.

### 2. Third-Party Services
The App uses **Google Firebase** (Analytics and Crashlytics) to collect the anonymous data described above. Such data is processed by Google under its privacy policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

### 3. How We Use the Data
We use the anonymous data solely to:
- Diagnose crashes and improve app stability;
- Understand feature usage and improve the product.

We do **not** use this data for ad targeting or cross-app tracking.

### 4. Your Choices and Rights
- **Disable telemetry**: You can turn off anonymous analytics and crash reporting in the App’s Settings. After disabling, we stop collecting such data.
- **Delete your data**: Since photos and videos always stay on your device, uninstalling the App removes the related material. Anonymous telemetry is not linked to your identity and requires no separate deletion.

### 5. Children’s Privacy
The App is rated **4+** and is not directed to children under 13, nor does it collect personal information from children.

### 6. Data Security
Your photos and videos always remain on your device; the App never uploads them to any server. Anonymous telemetry is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections to Firebase.

### 7. Changes
If this Privacy Policy changes, we will update it on this page and indicate the effective date.

### 8. Contact Us
For any questions, contact us at **privacy@stellarlink.app**.
